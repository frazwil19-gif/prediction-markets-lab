## 3. Cards — referee data audit
Pre-registered in `797b443`. Output: `cards/REFEREE_AUDIT.json`. Script: `scripts/cards_referee_audit.py`.

**Data:** free Football-Data raw CSVs already held for E0, E1 and SC0, 2020-21 to 2025-26. That is 6,960 matches.

**Coverage:** **0% missing** in every division-season.

**Identity:**
- 121 raw names normalise to 119 referees. The only merges were whitespace and case: `J Gillett`, and `K Stroud`/`k Stroud`.
- Names are initial-plus-surname. 17 surname groups have several initials (e.g. C/D/S Steven) and are listed for manual review. Most are probably distinct people, but a typo-split or a collision cannot be ruled out from this data.
- 36 referees officiate in more than one division.

**Matches per referee:**
- median 48, 90th percentile 138;
- 72 referees have at least 20 matches, and together they cover 96% of matches.

**Stability:** a referee's relative card rate in season t correlates with season t+1 at **r = 0.245** (229 referee-season pairs, p = 0.0002). The signal is real but noisy; the standard deviation of the referee-season relative rate is 0.57 cards per match.

**Out-of-sample test** (pre-set, one run; train before 2023-07, test 3,417 matches):
- Adding team rolling cards (R1) beats the league mean (R0): −0.0125 log loss at O3.5 and −0.0103 at O4.5.
- Adding the shrunken referee rate (R2 vs R1):

| Line | Δlogloss R2 − R1 | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| O3.5 | −0.0025 | [−0.0060, +0.0008] |
| O4.5 | −0.0060 | [−0.0092, −0.0031] |

- The referee coefficient is 0.69 on the log rate ratio.
- **Pre-set rule (CI upper < 0 at both lines): NOT established.** The effect is directionally positive at both lines and significant at O4.5. This is recorded as such, not rounded up.

**Settlement mismatch:**
- Football-Data's HY/AY/HR/AR record a second yellow as a red. The documentation does not say whether that player's first yellow stays in HY.
- Bookmaker "total cards" markets differ on whether a second yellow counts as 1 or 2 cards and on cards shown to non-players.
- "Booking points" markets (10 per yellow, 25 per red, often capped at 35 per player) are a different target altogether.
- Labels must therefore be mapped per venue before any cards model is evaluated against prices.
